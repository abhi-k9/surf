Why the edge star has a signed direction

The compact two-macro D–Y edge-star space has dimension 8. Every function in it vanishes on its shared interior edge. If such a function is nonnegative, the edge is a local minimum, forcing its first-normal derivative to vanish. That condition removes one dimension. The earlier archived certificate explicitly constructs 7 independent positive modes spanning the remaining subspace.

W = ker(Dₙ at midpoint) ⊕ span{signed mode}
dim(W) = 8, dim(ker) = 7

The complete eight-dimensional analytic edge star cannot have a fully nonnegative compact-support basis. This is an exact local impossibility statement—not a failure of numerical optimization.
